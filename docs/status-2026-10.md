## Q3 — SIC section

**Answer.** The review ("no SIC-section derivation exists") is **wrong** — it exists, and the
earlier "wrong column" bug was fixed.
- Derivation: `sic_section_from_code()` (a fixed, published SIC-2007 division→section lookup) in
  `validation/labels.py`, used on the label side. The snapshot side reuses the same helper via
  `snapshot_sic_section()` in `validation/control.py`, which parses the leading numeric out of the
  snapshot's `"62012 - label"` SIC text.
- The "wrong column" bug: section was derived from `sic07_1_digit` (a single digit — too coarse to
  resolve a 2-digit division→section mapping). It was fixed by switching the label SIC source to
  `sic07_2_digit`.
- **Reusable for a feature: yes.** `sic_section_from_code()` is a pure, stateless function taking a
  1-or-2-digit code. A basic-company-info feature could take the leading two digits of each 5-digit
  profile SIC code (exactly as `snapshot_sic_section` already does) and call it. Caveat: it
  currently lives under `validation/` (evaluation code), so reuse means importing from there or
  relocating the helper into `derive.py`.

**Evidence.**
- Lookup + function: [src/ukcompany/validation/labels.py:26-70](../src/ukcompany/validation/labels.py#L26)
  (`_SECTION_RANGES`, `sic_section_from_code`). Snapshot-side reuse:
  `src/ukcompany/validation/control.py` (`snapshot_sic_section`, imported `sic_section_from_code`);
  exported in [src/ukcompany/validation/__init__.py:12-17](../src/ukcompany/validation/__init__.py#L12).
- The column fix: [docs/AUDIT.md:682](AUDIT.md#L682) ("the maintainer's switch of the label SIC

## Q5 — Accounts join prerequisite (per-filing date?)

**Answer.** The LONG and WIDE outputs carry **accounting-period dates only** (`period_end` and
`made_up_to_date`) — there is **no explicit per-filing filed/received date**. However, every LONG
row also carries the **source monthly archive** (`source_year`, `source_month`, `source_archive`,
`source_member`), which is a **month-resolution proxy** for when the filing was published to the
register (Companies House groups accounts into monthly bulk archives by publication). The WIDE table
surfaces this as `row_available_yyyymm`.
- **Implication:** a point-in-time join is possible at **month resolution** (via
  `source_year/source_month` → `row_available_yyyymm`) combined with the accounting `period_end`,
  but **not at exact filing-date resolution**. A true per-filing filed/received date is not
  captured and would have to come from the filing-history API, not this dataset.

**Evidence.**
- LONG columns: [src/ukcompany/accounts/extract.py:38-52](../src/ukcompany/accounts/extract.py#L38)
  (`period_end`, `source_year`, `source_month`, `source_archive`, `source_member`,
  `made_up_to_date`); `CREATE TABLE observations` at
  [extract.py:174-190](../src/ukcompany/accounts/extract.py#L174) (`period_end`, `made_up_to_date`
  — no `filed_on`/`received_on`).
- WIDE columns: [src/ukcompany/accounts/pivot.py:126-132](../src/ukcompany/accounts/pivot.py#L126)
  (`period_end`, `made_up_to_date`); full WIDE schema listed in
  [docs/accounts-kaggle-safety-checks.md:9-18](accounts-kaggle-safety-checks.md#L9)
  (`company`, `period_end`, nine totals, four members, `employees_unit_anomaly`,
  `n_concepts_present`, `n_source_filings`, `row_available_yyyymm` — no filing-date column).

**Confidence:** Checked (dates present/absent). Inferred (that `source_month`/`row_available_yyyymm`
equals a *publication* month — this follows from the monthly-archive design, but the field is the
archive month, not a stated CH filing date).
