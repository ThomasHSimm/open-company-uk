Indicators: validation

Summary

Validation asks a narrow, honest question: when a company is independently known to have had an adverse insolvency event, does the pipeline’s corresponding flag fire? It compares cached pipeline flags against Insolvency Service adverse-case labels, using a control cohort of active companies matched on structural features (industry and age) that exist before any distress. Because the published labels contain positives but no verified negatives, this is a positive-unlabelled study: it can measure conditional recall and whether signals separate from a similar control, but it cannot establish precision, a false-positive rate, or anything about “trustworthiness”. No representative result is published yet.

Details

Study design

The validation harness compares cached pipeline flags with Insolvency Service adverse-case labels. It is cache-only: sampling writes company-number lists, the operator fetches those lists explicitly, and evaluation then reads cached data. Recent positives are sampled because older labelled companies are often dissolved, purged, or recovered.

Conditional recall is the share of assessable labelled adverse companies for which the applicable insolvency signal fires. The denominator excludes records that cannot fairly be assessed, and every exclusion bucket is reported. Recall is also broken down by case type; solvent members’ voluntary liquidations do not count as adverse cases.

The primary result is the cohort disposition: how many labelled companies the current pipeline excludes as dissolved/closed, flags, misses, cannot find, or has not fetched. The positive cohort must be supplied explicitly during evaluation, preventing unrelated entries in the shared cache from entering the result. Conditional recall applies only to the currently screenable subset. Since the labels and register usually reflect the same filed insolvency event, high recall is expected and is not a predictive-performance claim.

The comparison cohort is drawn from active companies in the dated monthly snapshot and stratified to the positives by SIC section and coarse company-age band. Those are pre-distress structural confounders; downstream outcomes such as insolvency status, filing compliance, or charges are never matching dimensions.

Positive-unlabelled framing

The label publication contains positives, not verified negatives. The control is therefore unlabelled and merely assumed not to be labelled positive. Its flag rate is not precision and is not a false-positive rate. The design can measure conditional recall and whether signals separate from a structurally similar control; it cannot establish accuracy against negative ground truth or validate the broader concept of trustworthiness.
